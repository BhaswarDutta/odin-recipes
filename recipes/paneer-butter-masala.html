Paneer Butter Masala

Description

Paneer butter masala is a rich, creamy North Indian curry made with soft paneer cubes simmered in a buttery tomato-based gravy flavoured with aromatic spices. Finished with cream and a touch of sweetness, it pairs perfectly with naan, kulcha, or fragrant basmati rice.

Ingredients

  • For curry base
    • 2 tbsp cooking oil
    • 1 tsp butter
    • 1 tsp cumin
    • 1 inch cinnamon
    • 4 cloves
    • 1 pod black cardamom
    • 1 bay leaf
    • 2 onion sliced
    • 5 cloves garlic
    • 1 inch ginger
    • 3 tomatoes sliced
    • 3 tbsp cashew
    • 1 cup water
  • For curry
    • 2 tsp oil
    • 1 tbsp butter
    • 1/2 tsp turmeric
    • 1 tsp chilli powder
    • 1/2 tsp cumin powder
    • 1/2 tsp corainder powder
    • 1/2 tsp garam masala
    • 1 tsp salt
    • 1 tsp sugar
    • 2 tbsp cream
    • 20 cubes paneer
    • 2 tbsp kasuri methi, crushed
    • 2 tbsp coriander, finely chopped

Steps

  1. Firstly, in a wok, heat 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp butter, and sauté spices. I have added 1 tsp of cumin, 1 inch of cinnamon, 4 cloves, 1 pod of black cardamom, and 1 bay leaf.
  2. Once the spices have turned aromatic, add 2 onions, 5 cloves of garlic, and 1 inch of ginger, and sauté until the onions soften.
  3. Also add 3 tomatoes, 3 tbsp cashew and sauté for a minute.
  4. Now add 1 cup water, cover and boil for 5 to 10 minutes.
  5. Once the tomatoes are cooked well, discard the bay leaf and allow the mixture to cool completely.
  6. Now grind to a smooth paste. Filter out to get a silky smooth texture. Keep aside.
  7. In a wok, heat 2 tsp oil and 1 tbsp butter.
  8. Once the oil is heated, add ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder, ½ tsp cumin powder, ½ tsp coriander powder and ½ tsp garam masala.
  9. Sauté till the spices turn aromatic.
  10. Add in the prepared tomato onion base and cook well.
  11. Keep cooking and boil for 5 minutes, until the mixture is well-cooked.
  12. Now add 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp sugar, 2 tbsp cream and mix well.
  13. Add 20 cubes paneer and simmer for 5 minutes till the flavours are absorbed.
  14. Add 2 tbsp kasuri methi and 2 tbsp coriander. Mix well.
  15. Finally, enjoy Paneer Butter Masala Recipe with roti.
