Masala Kulcha

Description

  • Masala kulcha is a soft, fluffy Indian leavened flatbread stuffed with a spiced mixture of potatoes, onions, herbs, and aromatic Indian spices. Baked in a tandoor or cooked on a hot griddle until golden and slightly crisp, it’s often served hot with chole, chutney, or a dollop of butter.

    Ingredients

    • For dough
      • 2 cup wheat flour (maida)
      • 1 tsp sugar
      • 1/4 tsp baking soda
      • 1 tsp baking soda
      • 1/2 tsp salt
      • 3 tsp cooking oil
      • 1/4 cup curd (yogurt)
      • 3/4 cup warm water
    • For stuffing
      • 2 cups of boiled and mashed potato
      • 3/4 cup of paneer
      • 1/2 tsp of kashmiri red chilli powder
      • 1/2 tsp of garam masala
      • 1 tsp aamchur (dry mango powder)
      • 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
      • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dry fenugreek leaves)
      • 2 tbsp coriander leaves, finely chopped
      • 1/4 tsp salt
    • Other ingredients
      • 2 tsp black sesame seeds
      • 3 tbsp coriander leaves, finely chopped
      • 2 tbsp butter

    Steps

    1. Pinch a ball sized dough and roll to 5-inch.
    2. Place a ball sized prepared aloo paneer stuffing in the centre.
    3. Bring the edge to centre and start pleating.
    4. Also join the pleats together and secure tight.
    5. Reverse the ball and sprinkle some black sesame seeds and coriander leaves.
    6. Flip it over, making sure the coriander leaves and sesame seeds facing down.
    7. Gently roll to oval shape using a rolling pin.
    8. Brush with water over kulcha.
    9. Furthermore, slowly flip the kulcha and put it on hot tawa.
    10. Gently press the kulcha.
    11. Now after a minute, flip the tawa upside down and cook kulcha directly on flame till they turn golden brown and puffs up.
    12. Gently scrape the kulcha from the bottom and remove.
    13. Also brush some butter.
    14. Finally, serve masala kulcha or aloo paneer kulcha hot with your favourite curry like paneer butter masala.
